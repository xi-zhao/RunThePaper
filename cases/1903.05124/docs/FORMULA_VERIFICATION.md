# Formula verification

All ten formula objects required by the numerical package are independently
open. “Open” means that the expression was not merely transcribed: its algebra,
limiting behavior, or a small-system realization was checked before it was
allowed to feed a numerical target.

| Formula | Numerical role | Independent check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EQC001 | Naive loss-style decoupling bound | exponent and threshold condition derived directly |
| EQC002 | Tight bound with accessible measurement outcomes | Haar-contraction cancellation and limiting cases checked |
| EQC003 | Unitary frame potential | composite-depth reduction and Haar moments checked |
| EQC004 | Clifford trace from the fixed-Pauli kernel | kernel/nullity rule matched dense traces |
| EQC005 | Stabilizer subsystem entropy | rank identity matched product, Bell, GHZ, and dense small-system states |
| EQC006 | Entropy density and measurement change | timing, sign, normalization, and bounds fixed before simulation |
| EQC007 | Half-chain finite-size scaling | synthetic collapses recover known critical parameters |
| EQC008 | Tripartite mutual information scaling | seven-entropy identity and synthetic crossing/collapse checked |
| EQC009 | Capacity of the degradable measurement channel | block-diagonal entropy identity derived analytically |
| EQC010 | Logarithmic critical entropy | weighted fits recover synthetic logarithmic slopes |

The machine-readable result is
`outputs/checks/formula_verification.json`. Numerical arrays are generated by
the verified formulas, independent Clifford/stabilizer trajectories, and fits
of those trajectories. No paper image, digitized curve, or author data array is
accepted by the generation path.
