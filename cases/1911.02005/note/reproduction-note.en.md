# Dual noise spectroscopy with band-limited control

The finite-difference filter, CPMG and DPSS controls were recalculated. Reanalysis of 99 shared frequencies and 13 sets of three-axis observations recovers peaks near 7 and 11 kHz. The DPSS marker RMS discrepancies for the two platforms are 0.0240 and 0.0109 Hz. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevapplied.14.024021)

## Scope

Partial scientific reproduction. Shot-resolved readouts and spectral calibration are missing. The runnable entry point covers the single-tone and pulsed filter blocks with self-contained inputs.
