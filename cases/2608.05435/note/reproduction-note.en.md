# Convolutional quantum Fourier transform

Matrix propagation and explicit 100-qubit compilation verify the convolutional QFT. The three-angle 100-qubit circuit has 590 CX gates, and 28 small matrix comparisons agree to 8.17e-16. [Paper / 原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05435v2)
