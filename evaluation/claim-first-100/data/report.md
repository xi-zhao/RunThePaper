## Metric definition

`Reproduction Degree = successful Claim coverage × conditional Fidelity / 100`.

Conditional Fidelity is computed only for reproduced Checks with complete, lifecycle-eligible, primary scientific-region pixel evidence generated by an independent render. Missing scores are not imputed. The report therefore also gives lower and upper bounds that assign unmeasured successful Checks Fidelity 0 and 100, respectively.

Papers are aggregated with equal weight per numerical Claim; Checks do not receive equal corpus weight, so papers with many supplementary panels cannot dominate the headline result.
Each Check retains exactly one verified direct Claim. An explicitly declared higher-level candidate Claim may reuse that Check as supporting evidence; no prose or pixel similarity is used to infer such a relation.
The complete Check ledger also projects direct cause, root cause, and code-fault status from every linked Target. Filtered tables preserve every objective blocker and every attempted failure without hiding them in aggregate rates.
