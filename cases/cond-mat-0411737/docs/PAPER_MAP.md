# Paper Map — Kane and Mele (2005)

## Scope rule

The reproduction unit is the whole paper, not only its plotted figure.  The
authored scope is frozen in `figure_coverage.json`: five display items and ten
independent text-only quantitative claims, for 13 eligible quantitative targets
in total.  Three display-carried items are targeted (Fig. 1, Fig. 2(a),
Fig. 2(b)); two display items are schematic exclusions (the Fig. 1 inset and
Fig. 3).  External literature values such as the cited mobility and graphite
splitting comparisons are contextual exclusions rather than deferred blockers,
because they are not independently reconstructable from this paper's own
scientific inputs.

## Figure classification

- Main Fig. 1 band axes: numerical, fully generated from Eq. (6).  The lattice
  inset is a schematic and is not used as numerical input.
- Main Fig. 2(a-b): schematics that carry standalone quantitative transport
  labels.  Their printed current and conductance values are counted once at the
  subfigure level and independently generated by T008-T009.
- Main Fig. 3: Feynman diagram.  The equations and numerical conclusion it
  motivates are independently generated by T012-T013.

## Declared assumptions

The paper does not give the Fig. 1 ribbon width or momentum grid.  The formal
run therefore uses widths 16, 20, 24, 28 and 32, with width 32 as the rendered
scientific dataset, and 401 momenta.  These values were chosen by convergence,
not by fitting source pixels.  Armchair widths, Kubo/shell/disorder grids and
finite-cylinder factors are likewise declared falsification/convergence
choices.  The finite-Rashba Kubo quantity is a conventional-current proxy
because the exact externally cited conserved-current operator is not printed.
The paper also omits the numerical graphene
lattice constant and Fermi velocity used in its two rounded material estimates;
the standard values 2.46 angstrom and 1e6 m/s remain explicitly non-paper-exact.

## Access boundary

The numerical runner receives only the local implementation, configuration and
entrypoint.  It cannot read `raw/`, `references/`, original figures, author
code, author arrays or digitized points.  The original Fig. 1 is opened only
after the numerical CSV hash is frozen, inside the RenderContract/pixel channel.
