# Displaced-cat noise spectroscopy

Independent filter calculations and nonnegative spectrum inversion reproduce the third-harmonic suppression mechanism. A 2 ms, seven-flip calculation reduces the third-harmonic peak ratio from 12.01% to 0.0646%. [Paper / 原文](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevlett.126.250506)

## Scope

Published averages support shared peaks near 5.25 and 31.3–31.5 kHz. Original AOM phase commands and shot-level records remain open.
