## Core chain

1. Main Eq. 1 / Eq. S24 / Table S1 define the Bose-Hubbard Hamiltonian.
2. Fixed-number ladder-operator matrix elements produce finite Hermitian matrices.
3. Exact unitary evolution gives one- and two-particle states.
4. Densities, entropy, connected correlations, concurrence, correlators, and double occupancy follow algebraically.
5. Supplement Sec. IV.C adds printed disorder and detuning-renormalized hopping for S9-S10.
6. Supplement Sec. IV.E gives distribution fidelity and parameter bounds for S11; the exact random realization and collapse convention are not published.
