# Concurrent Clifford noise: retained discrepancy

This is a same-researcher diagnostic, not a whole-paper acceptance judgment.
The model is main Eq. (2), supplement Eqs. (8–11), and the left-to-right physical pulse order explicitly specified in Table S3. Identity lasts pi/Omega; Z pulses are instantaneous. The last of J Cliffords inverts the preceding J−1. Noise is constant within each Clifford, including its constituent pulses.

Write the noisy Clifford as C_g(delta)=C_g(0)[I−i delta A_g+O(delta²)], with A_g=i C_g† (dC_g/d delta)|0. For prefix U_(j−1), its error contribution is U_(j−1)† A_g U_(j−1). The first-order state-transition amplitude is the transverse (X,Y) component of the sum. Thus the delta² coefficient of infidelity is |sum_j a_j,xy|² for DC noise, but sum_j |a_j,xy|² for independent zero-mean noise at each Clifford. Cross terms cannot be discarded just because the ideal Cliffords are uniform: each error generator depends on the same Clifford that rotates the next error.

Interaction-frame integration of every physical pulse, independently of the closed-form pulse propagator, gives mean |A_g|² = 0.6028083791780142 and adjacent isotropic dot product = 0.15208358043227554. The Clifford twirl of the mean generator has norm 6.51e−17; separated bulk correlations vanish after the intervening independent Clifford twirl. Consequently the bulk white coefficient is (2/3)*0.6028083791780142 = 0.4018722527853428, matching Table S2. The bulk DC coefficient retains twice the adjacent cross term and becomes 0.6046503600283768.

A second method uses Frechet derivatives of matrix exponentials and propagates probabilities, first moments and second transverse moments on the 24 accumulated Clifford states. The inverse recovery is appended explicitly. At J=200 this gives DC coefficient 0.6033735204270998 and white coefficient 0.4011868635908227, normalized by J sigma². At J=25,50,100,200 the finite-length DC values approach the bulk limit. Independent finite-difference Monte Carlo and direct small-detuning transition amplitudes agree within their stated Monte Carlo uncertainty; see checks.json and diagnosis.json.

The reported Table S2 coefficient is 0.4018722527853427 for both noise types. Under this literal implementation the DC leading coefficient is about 50% larger. This survives reducing detuning, so finite J sigma² corrections alone do not explain it. Independent sign randomization removes the cross term and restores the white bulk value, a discriminating control. Reversing pulse order and halving idle time were explored as convention controls, not accepted replacements for the source model. No author array or code supplied the independent output.

This is a paper-formula discrepancy candidate under the stated pulse/noise convention, not proof of what the laboratory actually implemented. No claim is made that the raw RB ion data or exact experimental sequence list were reproduced. The subsequent table_s3_audit.json verifies every printed source action and duration. Remaining scientific work includes recorded per-proposition checks; a mismatch in one coefficient does not adjudicate the whole histogram, variance formula, or experimental figure.
