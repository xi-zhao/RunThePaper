# Paper Map

## Scientific core

The paper introduces a five-step driven bipartite square lattice and the gap
winding invariant of the full time evolution.  It also develops a frequency-
domain truncated Floquet Hamiltonian and demonstrates anomalous edge modes in a
weakly driven two-band model.

## Printed parameters

- square drive: five equal steps, `T=1`, `delta_AB=0.5 pi/T`, and
  `J=(0.5,1.5,2.5) pi/T`; ideal point `JT/5=pi/2`, `delta_AB=0`;
- weak drive: `mu=1`, `J/mu=b/mu=1.5`, `a/mu=4`, `Delta0/mu=1`,
  `Delta0/omega=0.07`, and the harmonic perturbation
  `Delta0 sigma_z cos(omega t)`;
- the strip width, k meshes, phase-diagram scan mesh, Floquet truncation used in
  Fig. 6(c), and numerical tolerances are not printed.  The text analyzes
  `M=1`; that value is used as the declared reconstruction for Fig. 6(c).
