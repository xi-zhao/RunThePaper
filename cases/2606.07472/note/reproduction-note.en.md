# Quasi-zero exchange pulses

The four-pole voltage transfer and exchange model recover drift reduction for the tested repetition window. Two common-timing fits give reference fringe errors of 0.1395 and 0.0718 mV. [Paper / 原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07472v1)

## Scope

The historical pulse timing and Tunnel Falls calibration are unavailable. The fits vary with the timing constraints; the released runner evaluates the independent pulse model.

The results shown here come from the verified version 11 of this partial reproduction.
