# Methods

The current methods are in Section 3 of [the complete manuscript](paper.md#study-design-and-evidence-model). The study uses one fixed cohort of 100 papers, with measurement-specific availability and provenance. The former standalone draft has been superseded to avoid conflicting descriptions of study groups.

The extraction reads frozen public Git objects, does not execute scientific cases, and retains unresolved references and unknown scientific outcomes. Selection provenance remains in `../cohort.json`; it does not define manuscript comparison groups. Current statistics, input hashes and analysis units are in [figure_data.json](figure_data.json) and [claim_evidence.csv](claim_evidence.csv).
