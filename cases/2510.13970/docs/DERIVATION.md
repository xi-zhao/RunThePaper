# Rendered equations

## `EQ_SYMMETRY`

$$
P_A\rho_A(t)P_A=\rho_A(t),\qquad
w_+(t)-w_-(t)=\operatorname{Tr}[\rho_A(t)P_A].
$$

## `EQ_EFFECTIVE`

$$
H_{\mathrm{vV}}=A+\frac{[B,[A,B]]}{4\omega^2},\qquad
H_F(0)=A+\frac{[B,[A,B]]/4-[A,[A,B]]}{\omega^2}+O(\omega^{-3}).
$$

## `EQ_SPEED`

$$
\|[A,P_A]\|_\infty=2JN_{\mathrm{cut}},\qquad
t_{\min}\ge \frac{1}{2JN_{\mathrm{cut}}}.
$$

## `EQ_MISMATCH`

$$
\|H_F-H_{\mathrm{gold}}\|_\infty
=\frac{101.33438627528581}{\omega^2}+O(\omega^{-3}).
$$
