# Fixed-v2 reconstruction and independent checks

Source: supplied cond-mat/0604476v2 PDF and three-member arXiv source archive; no author computation is used. J=hbar=a=1. Numerical boundary conditions are hard walls, as explicitly stated in the main text. The periodic expressions in equations (5)-(8) must be adapted to standing-wave occupations for those calculations.

## Dynamics and observable
The OBC one-body Hamiltonian is h_ij=-(delta_{i,j+1}+delta_{i+1,j}). Its eigenvectors U_im=sqrt(2/(L+1))sin(pi*i*m/(L+1)), i,m=1..L, have energies -2cos(pi*m/(L+1)). For the box quench, embed the 30 lowest eigenvectors of the Lin=150 box at sites 226..375 of L=600. For the superlattice quench, occupy the 30 lowest eigenvectors of h+8cos(pi*i/2), then propagate with h. No continuum, local-density or time-stepping approximation is made: P(t)=U exp(-i e t) U^T P(0).

The correlation needed is rho_ij=<b_i^dagger b_j>, not <c_i^dagger c_j>. For i<j, the Jordan-Wigner string on sites I={i+1,...,j-1} gives rho_ij=det(A) P_j A^{-1} P_i^dagger, with A=1-2 P_I^dagger P_I. The numerically nonsingular formulation is the negative bordered determinant -det([[A,P_i^dagger],[P_j,0]]). It remains valid when A is singular. Diagonal rho_ii=sum_m |P_im|^2; enforce Hermitian symmetry. f(k)=L^{-1}[N+2 Re sum_{d=1}^{L-1} exp(-2pi i k d/L) sum_i rho_{i,i+d}]. The full L discrete momenta sum to N. Exact finite systems need not converge pointwise as t->infinity: the paper's near-stationarity is a finite-time, observable-dependent statement.

## Gaussian ensembles
n_m=sum_a |(U^T P)_ma|^2 are conserved standing-wave occupations. The maximum-entropy Gaussian ensemble has independent Bernoulli modes, lambda_m=log((1-n_m)/n_m), Z=product_m(1+exp(-lambda_m)). Its one-body C=U diag(n_m) U^T determines hard-core correlations by Wick's theorem: rho_ij=det(1-2 C_II)[C_ij+2 C_iI(1-2 C_II)^{-1} C_Ij]. A growing Schur-complement evaluation is checked against explicit mixtures of every fermionic Fock eigenstate for L=6. Correlation cutoffs 160/240 (and 240/360 for the sampled Lin range) quantify omitted long-distance correlations, rather than assuming them zero.

Grand canonical occupations are 1/(1+exp(alpha+beta*e_m)), with alpha,beta fitted simultaneously to the conserved N and energy. Compare bosonic distributions from the same Wick strings. Particle-number fluctuations of this ensemble and GGE are intentional; actual dynamics has fixed N.

## Analytic assessment
Varying S/k_B=-Tr rho log rho with normalization and fixed <I_m> gives log rho=-1-eta-sum lambda_m I_m. Concavity, or relative entropy D(rho||rho_GGE)>=0, independently establishes maximality among density matrices obeying these mean constraints. Commuting 0/1 occupations factor the partition function and give n_m=(1+exp(lambda_m))^{-1}; n=0,1 are limiting projectors. These equations do not prove that unitary dynamics converges or that the mode-occupation GGE predicts every nonlocal observable. Conserved products n_m n_n contain additional initial correlations; the paper tests the bosonic one-body distribution.

OBC Jordan-Wigner mapping removes every nearest-neighbor string exactly. A periodic boundary crossing contributes (-1)^(N+1), yielding periodic fermions for odd N and antiperiodic for even N. Fermionic CAR are {c_i,c_j^dagger}=delta_ij. Fixed v2 instead prints 1 for all i,j in the line under Eq.(5): literally false for i!=j, a local algebraic typo. This correction is explicit and is not used to conceal a numerical mismatch.

For a periodic parity sector with allowed physical momenta q, (1-cos q)^2=3/2-2cos q+(1/2)cos(2q). Thus sum_q(1-cos q)^2 n_q=3N/2+H/J+(1/4)sum_i(c_i^dagger c_{i+2}+h.c.). The inverse mapping gives c_i^dagger c_{i+2}=b_i^dagger(1-2n_{i+1})b_{i+2}, proving the stated string identity. Eq.(7)'s weight contains an undefined n while summing k; its meaningful form uses q (including the half-integer shift for even N). For OBC, simply transplanting the periodic fourth-moment identity omits boundary terms. No OBC fourth-moment identity is assumed in generation.

## Decision criteria, fixed before full results
Small-system exact many-body correlation checks: max absolute error <=1e-10, enough to reject missing strings and incorrect phases. Normalization/orthonormality <=1e-9 at L=600. Compare independent direct bordered determinants with the rank-one implementation. Report absolute and L1/N GGE differences, late-time fluctuations and thermal discrepancies separately; no universal theorem is inferred from small residuals. Original EPS coordinates permit full vector curve comparison; coordinate rounding, x-grid interpolation and color-map sampling are separate reference errors. No curve coordinate enters independent generation. A reduced discrepancy alone will not identify the authors' implementation.
