# Heralded photonic graph states

Fourier coefficients, sparse creation and matrix permanents agree on exact Fock-sector propagation. The paper's five-mode four-photon Bell example gives success probability 1/9 and unit conditional fidelity. Small graph classes and compilation identities were also checked. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1103/c4v4-wz65)
