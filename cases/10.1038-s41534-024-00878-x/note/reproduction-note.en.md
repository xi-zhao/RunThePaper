# Quantum-dot tuning data and metrics

An analysis of the QFlow data discussed in the workshop report tests transfer across devices. The mixed-scan split gives 50.00% accuracy, and the two held-out device directions give 8.19% and 9.40%. [Paper / 原文](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41534-024-00878-x) · [Data / 资料](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/qflow-2-0-generating-a-dataset-for-automating-quantum-dot-device-tuning)

## Scope

This contribution is a workshop synthesis. The public result reanalyzes its cited evidence; online tuning requires historical actions and measurement sequences.

The results shown here come from the verified version 17 of this partial reproduction.
