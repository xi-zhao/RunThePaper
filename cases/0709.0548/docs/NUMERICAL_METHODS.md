# Numerical Methods

The production run evaluates two independent 500-member `U(32)` ensembles: complex-Gaussian QR-Haar matrices and a transparent depth-32 local brickwork circuit made from Haar `SU(2)` gates and alternating controlled-Z layers. For every matrix it freezes all 32 eigenphases and all 41 discord values after minimizing the paper's exact conditional entropy over 129 projective axes. The paper does not publish its finite pseudorandom circuit, depth, seed, or matrices, so neither ensemble is misreported as an exact reconstruction of those hidden instances.

Small dense matrices independently validate the DQC1 block-state spectrum, entropy, normalized-trace readout, and opening separable example. Control/register separability is not inferred from PPT: the runner reconstructs the state explicitly as a convex mixture of product states at five stages of a continuous unitary path. Every grouped-control bipartition through five register qubits is separately evaluated with PPT negativity and the realignment norm; non-detection is not interpreted as proof of separability.

The root-of-unity approximation is swept over the entire finite phi cell for dimensions 4 through 4096. This exposes the paper's literal finite-grid phi-independence as false while measuring its valid continuum limit. The cited first Doherty symmetric-extension problem is emitted as an exact machine-readable SDP contract, but remains unexecuted because the publication provides no solver/certificate and the environment has no PSD-cone solver.

The full CPU campaign takes seconds and requires no GPU or sharding. Paper-stated `n=5` and 500 instances are executed; only unpublished generator details remain unrecoverable.
