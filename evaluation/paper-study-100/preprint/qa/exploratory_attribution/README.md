# Withdrawn exploratory attribution

These four files preserve an exploratory mapping of diagnostic labels to responsibility domains. They are not manuscript results and are excluded from the submission package. The mapping mixes physical limitations with workflow maintenance and does not apply a uniform causal-adjudication protocol. Its counts must not be cited as the prevalence of paper errors, Agent failures, compute shortages or missing equipment.

The current manuscript uses source-linked illustrative diagnostic narratives in `anc/obstacle_examples.json`. The original diagnostic records remain available separately. No scientific case status has been changed.
