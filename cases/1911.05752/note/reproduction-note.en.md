# Adaptive filtering of projective measurements

Nine panels were recalculated with five particle settings, 50 trials and 75 steps. Late slope point estimates agreed in direction under the archive implementation, with both intervals supporting the reported direction in three panels. Two truncated-Gaussian slopes change sign after avoiding weight underflow. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1103/physreva.104.012412)
