# 1202.5532: Growth of entanglement entropy in a free-fermion model

## What was reproduced

Independent free-fermion ensembles, U(1) MPS and convergence controls support logarithmic entanglement growth and volume-law saturation.

## Boundary and remaining work

Finite-size and numerical-convergence boundaries are retained; entropy extrapolation is not a formal thermodynamic proof.
