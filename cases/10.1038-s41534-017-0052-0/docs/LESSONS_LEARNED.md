# Calculation checks / 复现札记

Compute first-order Pauli-space moments with exact transitions of all 24 Cliffords and the terminal inverse. Compare quasistatic and independent errors under the same sequence convention. Frozen GST fit summaries remain separate from the RB calculation.

Original RB and detection records, laboratory conventions and the original Hessian intervals remain unresolved. The public entry point reruns finite-state RB moments; original GST counts are available from the authors.
