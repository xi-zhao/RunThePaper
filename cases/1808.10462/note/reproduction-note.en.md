# Phase-modulated robust entangling gates

The four-segment analytic phases close at all 121 points of the Figure 3 scan. Figure 4 squared-displacement exponents are 2.0066, 4.0014 and 5.9988. The calculations cover dynamic noise and 2–10 ions, including solutions for all 45 ten-ion pairs at 105–175 μs. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevapplied.13.024022)
