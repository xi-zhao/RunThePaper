# Paper Map

## Scientific argument

The paper derives an active vertex model, maps flow regimes across shape and
activity, relates shear thickening to stress fluctuations and networks, and
proposes viscosity/onset scaling. A full reproduction must independently
generate every curve, fit, marker, boundary, field, distribution, network, and
claim-level convergence or analytic check.
