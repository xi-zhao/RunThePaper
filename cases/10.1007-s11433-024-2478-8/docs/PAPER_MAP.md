## Assumptions

- Buffer atom always in `|1>`; qubit `|0>` is dark; qubit atoms share drive
  `(Ω₂, Δ₂)`, buffer uses `(Ω₁, Δ₁)` (homogeneous driving within each species).
- Control–target direct interaction is negligible; only adjacent pairs interact.
- Rydberg dipole–dipole modelled as a Förster resonance `|rr'>↔|qq'>` with
  strength `B`, penalty `δ_q = 0`. `B = 2π·50 MHz` for Fig. 3.
- Triply-excited three-body states are blockaded and excluded from `H_d`.
- Rotating-frame sign convention taken directly from the appendix
  (`+Δ|e><e|`); confirmed correct because it reproduces the paper's gate error.
