## Reproduction Goal

This case has ten declared quantitative targets:

- two display-carried items: Main Fig. 1 scientific geometry/extrema (T003-T004)
  and the Main Fig. 2 caption/main-text DQC1 claims (T005-T006);
- six text-only targets with no dedicated display item: T001, T002, T007,
  T008, T009, and T010. T009-T010 have executable isolated runners, but remain
  excluded from scientific scoring because the publication does not specify the
  computational-hardness contract or the entanglement measure/tolerance needed
  for a paper-exact decision.

Definitions such as Shannon entropy, mutual information, and conditional-state
notation remain derivation dependencies, not standalone reproduction targets.
