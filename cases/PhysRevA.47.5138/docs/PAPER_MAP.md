## Reproduction Goal

Reproduce every numerical panel from the collective-spin definitions and
Hamiltonians: the six S=20 Husimi-Q distributions in Figs. 2(a--c) and 3(a--c),
and all scientific series in Fig. 4. Source pixels may be used only after
numerical data are frozen, for comparison and presentation tuning.

Figs. 1 and 5 are conceptual schematics, not numerical targets.

## Assumptions and Unprinted Presentation Choices

- QPD means the paper's `Q(theta,phi)=|<theta,phi|psi>|^2`, normalized by its
  independently computed maximum only for rendering.
- The scientific parameters S and mu are printed exactly. Angular mesh density,
  sphere grid spacing, rasterizer and the exact set of S markers in Fig. 4 are
  not printed; they are presentation/sampling choices, not fitted physics.
- Fig. 4's two-axis points are generated by exact finite-dimensional unitary
  evolution and minimization, not copied from the graph.
